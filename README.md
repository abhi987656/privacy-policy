<h1>Paisa privacy policy</h1>
<p class="meta">Paisa: Expense Tracker &amp; Budget (<code>com.drabhishekrajput.paisa</code>), by Abhishek Rajput.<br>
Effective 11 October 2026.</p>

<p class="summary"><strong>In short:</strong> Paisa keeps everything you enter on your phone. The app
has no internet permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere. There are no accounts, ads,
analytics or tracking.</p>

<h2>What the app stores</h2>
<p>Paisa stores what you type into it in a database on your device:</p>
<ul>
  <li>your income and expense entries: amount, date and time, category, payment method, note and cashback;</li>
  <li>your categories, payment methods, monthly budgets and the "To buy" list;</li>
  <li>your app settings, such as theme, currency and decimal places.</li>
</ul>
<p>Paisa does not ask for your name, phone number, email address, contacts, location, photos or
bank details, and it does not read your SMS messages or other apps' data.</p>

<h2>What leaves your phone</h2>
<p><strong>Nothing, unless you do it yourself.</strong> Paisa has no internet permission and no
third-party code that collects data (no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs). The
developer never receives or sees your data.</p>
<p>Your data leaves the app only in these cases, which you control:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Export backup / Export transactions as CSV.</strong> When you tap these, Android asks
    you where to save the file (for example Downloads or Google Drive). The file goes only where
    you choose. It is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe.</li>
  <li><strong>Import backup.</strong> Paisa reads only the file you pick and uses it to replace the
    data in the app, after you confirm.</li>
  <li><strong>Android's own backup.</strong> If backup is turned on in your phone's settings,
    Android can include Paisa's database in your device backup to your Google account and copy it
    to a new phone during setup. This is a feature of Android, handled by Google under your
    account's settings. You can turn it off in your phone's backup settings.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Sharing</h2>
<p>Paisa does not share, sell or transfer your data to anyone, because it never receives it.</p>

<h2>Deleting your data</h2>
<ul>
  <li>In the app: Settings → Data → <em>Delete all transactions</em>, <em>Clear the wish list</em>
    and <em>Reset preferences</em>.</li>
  <li>Uninstalling Paisa, or clearing its storage in Android's app settings, deletes everything
    the app stored on the device.</li>
  <li>Backup files you exported stay where you saved them until you delete them.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Children</h2>
<p>Paisa is a general personal finance tool and is not directed at children. It collects no
personal information from anyone.</p>

<h2>Changes to this policy</h2>
<p>If a future version of Paisa handles data differently, this page will be updated before that
version is released, and the effective date above will change.</p>
